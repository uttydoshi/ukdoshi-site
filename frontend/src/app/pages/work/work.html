How I work

Why
People do their best work when they feel safe, trusted and heard. In the age of AI, remembering that we’re all human matters more than ever.
How
  • Bring people together around clear direction, goals and roadmaps
  • Shield the team from noise so they can do their best work
  • Make it safe to make mistakes and learn from them
  • Give honest, constructive feedback, and ask for it in return
  • Facilitate rather than dictate: rituals, workshops and team discussions where everyone has a voice
  • Lead with care, and help each other
What

I lead engineering, DevOps and AI platform teams. Most recently, I led the AI platform that powers Culture Amp’s AI features.

  • Manage and grow the people in my teams
  • Own delivery end to end, from roadmap to production
  • Own the technical direction: solution design and key decisions
  • Make work visible with dashboards I build myself, covering delivery stats, roadmaps, LLM usage and AI model deprecations
  • Ask good questions and play devil’s advocate, so we land on solid decisions where everyone feels heard
  • Stay hands-on, coding with Claude Code when the work needs it
