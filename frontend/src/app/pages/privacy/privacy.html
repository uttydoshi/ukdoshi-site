Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ukdoshi.com is a personal website. This page explains what information it collects and how that information is used.

Browsing the site

You can read the public pages without signing in. The site does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), which processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages to you.

Signing in

Some areas need you to sign in with your Google account. Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with this site. The site never sees your Google password, and no other password is created or stored.

This information is used only to sign you in and to decide whether you have access to the Inner Circle. It is stored with AWS in the United States. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in until you sign out or the token expires.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone else, except with the service providers that run the site (AWS, and Google for signing in).

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your information at any time by emailing utty@ukdoshi.com.
