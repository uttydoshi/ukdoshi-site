Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

ukdoshi.com is a personal website. This page explains what information it collects and how that information is used.

Browsing the site

You can read the public pages without signing in. The site does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), which processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver pages to you.

Signing in

Some areas need you to sign in, either with Google or with an email address.

  • With Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with this site. The site never sees your Google password.
  • With email: the site stores your email address and sends a one-time code to it. No password is created or stored.

This information is used only to sign you in and to decide whether you have access to restricted areas. It is stored with AWS in the United States. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in until you sign out or the token expires.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone else, except with the service providers that run the site (AWS, and Google if you sign in with Google).

Your choices

You can ask to see or delete your information at any time by emailing utty@ukdoshi.com.
